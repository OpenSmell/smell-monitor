A repeatable, controlled headspace presented to swappable gas sensors. Every phase is animated so a Seeed engineer can verify clearances, assembly order, plug-in, and the Osmograph QC loop without opening a CAD file.

The shell is an open PETG tray (122 × 114 × 43 mm) + flat 6 mm screwed lid, four M3 screws into heat-set inserts. No baffle, no plenum, no disposable liner. Wetted faces are PTFE-lined — no smell memory builds up inside the chamber.

  • Shell PETG FDM / AL 6061-T6 substitute · lid same spool
  • Board on four Ø6 standoffs; top face = chamber floor
  • Filter drawer starts 10.8 mm behind front wall (clears lid bosses)
  • Fan outside rear face on 4 mm plate — motor heat never enters sample

Assembly order: tray → PCB on standoffs → 8 cartridges → filter pad → lid → Osmograph. Service: lid off, modules lift straight out, ≤ 60 s, no tools.

Eight identical slots: keyed 5 V gas-cell socket (+5V GND AO DO 3V3 ID) + 3.3 V Qwiic/STEMMA QT port at every slot. Cartridges mount flat — right-angle header in a vertical socket, sensing face up — so a MOX can's end-face inlet points at its window, not its neighbour.

MOX row last: heaters (~0.9 W/slot) and VOC sink sit downstream of everything so purge carries desorbed gas straight out the exhaust.
